B2B Paid Search

Rebuilding a National B2B Paid Search Program After Agency Transition

📆 July 2025 – Present 📍 National 🌍 Google Search, Performance Max, Display
10x+ Revenue per lead YoY
50%+ YoY lead growth
4.0x Marketing ROI

The Challenge

Inherited an account heavily reliant on Performance Max (approximately 90% of spend) with branded keywords inflating reported conversions. Leadership had no clear view into what was actually driving non-branded demand. Conversion tracking inconsistencies made month-over-month comparison unreliable.

What I Did

  • Separated branded vs. non-branded search into dedicated campaigns
  • Repositioned Performance Max as a secondary, governed channel
  • Excluded branded keywords from PMax to prevent performance inflation
  • Implemented Dynamic Keyword Insertion and location personalization
  • Standardized UTM parameters across all SEM for GA4 alignment
  • Audited and corrected form submission tracking errors
  • Built a negative keyword list to eliminate wasted spend
  • Initiated Salesforce to Google Ads closed-loop reporting alignment

Paid Search Turnaround

Reactivating a Stalled Account for a National Logistics Provider

📆 November 2024 – Present 🌍 Google Ads 📍 National
+50% Conversions MoM
−20% Cost per acquisition

The Challenge

When the account was inherited in November, ad spend was significantly under target due to restrictive budget settings. Conversions were steadily declining and conversion tracking contained duplicates, including a page view counted as a primary conversion, making performance data unreliable.

What I Did

  • Raised Target CPAs to reduce lost impression share to competitors
  • Switched campaigns to Maximize Clicks to re-activate volume
  • Removed device bid adjustments to let Google optimize naturally
  • Turned off Display Network on Search campaigns
  • Paused high-spend, low-converting keywords
  • Cleaned conversion tracking, removed duplicates and page view primary
  • Added Dynamic Search Ads for broader coverage
  • Implemented keyword and location insertion in ad copy

ABM Strategy & Execution

Full-Funnel ABM Campaign for Government Technology Company Targeting State DOTs Nationally

📆 September 2025 – Present 🌍 LinkedIn, StackAdapt, HubSpot Email 📍 National — State Departments of Transportation
47 DOT accounts reached
3 Channels coordinated

The Challenge

The client offers two distinct products targeting state Departments of Transportation: a Product A management platform and a labor compliance and certified payroll reporting solution. Government buyers operate on long procurement cycles, make decisions by committee, and rarely fill out forms early in their journey. The challenge was building a coordinated ABM program that could generate measurable engagement across a highly specific named account list, with no paid search intent to rely on, while surfacing meaningful buying signals for the sales team.

What I Did

  • Designed the full 3-stage campaign strategy: audience building, engagement and nurture, retargeting and conversion
  • Built and maintained the named account target list across state DOT agencies nationally
  • Launched coordinated LinkedIn and StackAdapt ABM campaigns for both B2Gnow and eComply product tracks simultaneously
  • Wrote LinkedIn ad copy, InMail sequences, and 4-email nurture series for each product track
  • Configured HubSpot workflows to enroll contacts after content downloads and trigger sales alerts on warm leads
  • Built client-facing interactive reporting deliverables: account-level engagement tables, cross-platform bar charts, and video completion reports by named DOT account
  • Tracked post-ad intent signals (demo page visits without a click) as the primary buying signal for government accounts
  • Proposed and designed a focused 1:1 content strategy for prioritizing high-value individual DOT targets

Beyond Execution

AI-Powered Marketing Systems

When standard tools hit their ceiling, I build what's missing. These are three prompt-driven systems I designed from scratch: one to solve a specific client reporting problem that no dashboard could fix, and two deployed agency-wide to raise the baseline of what strategy work looks like at scale.

Client-Specific

Account-Level ABM Reporting System

A government technology client needed board-level visibility into which named accounts were engaging across LinkedIn, StackAdapt, and HubSpot. The problem: no existing dashboard could surface account-level intent signals, email engagement, and video completion data in one view, in a format leadership could actually read. I made a product decision: instead of patching together screenshots from three platforms, I designed a structured prompt system that transforms raw multi-platform exports into a custom interactive HTML deliverable, sortable by account, signal type, and engagement depth. No third-party software required to open or present it.

The problem it solved Standard dashboards couldn't connect account identity across platforms or present cross-channel engagement in a format meaningful for board reporting
What it produces Interactive, sortable account engagement tables, cross-platform intent charts, video completion data by named account, and intent signal callouts
Why it matters Turns hours of manual export reconciliation into a repeatable, client-ready deliverable that makes ABM performance legible at the executive level
Personal Workflow

Demand Generation Strategy Generator

Building a full demand generation strategy from scratch was taking 10 to 15 hours per client. I had all the right inputs, personas, pain points, problems, competitive context, but synthesizing them into a comprehensive, creative program was time-intensive and left room for blind spots. I built a structured prompt that takes everything I know about a client and produces a complete three-phase demand gen program. It surfaces channel combinations and tactical angles I might not have reached on my own, in a fraction of the time.

The problem it solved Strategy development was taking 10 to 15 hours per engagement. Structured inputs existed but synthesizing them into a full program manually left gaps and took too long
What it produces Deck-ready three-phase demand gen program: paid media plan, ABM layer, email strategy, content recommendations, geo targeting, and event and publication suggestions
Why it matters Compresses strategy development from days to hours and consistently surfaces ideas that pure manual thinking misses
Agency-Wide

Sales Enablement Leave-Behind Generator

B2B clients with field sales teams kept asking for leave-behind ideas and the requests were handled ad hoc, starting from scratch every time. I built a reusable prompt that generates a complete tiered leave-behind strategy on demand: from high-volume cold visit materials to premium ABM gifting, with persona-specific concepts, trade show ideas, event tactics, and creative names and taglines built in. Every output follows the same scalable formula so the quality is consistent regardless of industry.

The problem it solved Sales enablement requests were recurring but handled inconsistently, with no repeatable starting point for creative concepting across client types
What it produces Tiered leave-behind program from cold outreach through premium ABM gifting, with persona-targeted concepts, QR code CTAs, trade show tactics, and event ideas
Why it matters Fully reusable across any industry by swapping the client brief, giving sales teams a strategic, creative starting point in minutes instead of days
